---
name: Live team identity authority
description: Distinguish harmless provider labels from financial instrument substitutions.
---

Accept exact provider-supplied aliases for the same franchise, but require commissioner confirmation before replacing a sold pool entry with a different franchise to match a live bracket.

**Why:** An imported auction roster can disagree with provider participants. Treating an unmatched franchise as a renamed entry would reassign financial exposure without authorization, even when the losing team's present points happen to be zero.

**How to apply:** Keep ownership and sale economics unchanged while investigating. Fail actuals closed on missing or ambiguous participants, explain the mismatch, and obtain explicit confirmation of any scoped entry-identity correction.
