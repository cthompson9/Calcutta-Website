Implementation
1. Create one pure helper for canonical quote normalization and eligibility. It should return:
   - Observation identity and ticker.
   - Role: active_price, settlement_fact, or unusable.
   - Whether it is used in fitting.
   - Exclusion reasons.
   - The transformed price, bounds, or settlement value passed to fitting.
   - Capture time and source provenance.
   Make input derivation, diagnostic persistence, and publication checks consume this shared decision.
2. Preserve Kalshi’s updated_time as market-metadata update time. Do not treat it as the time of the latest bid/ask observation.
3. Record capture time when a current-market response is successfully received. When no verified trading-event timestamp is available, use this capture time as the observation time of the returned book. Keep metadata-update time separate.
   Do not give replayed or cached saved responses a new capture timestamp. Missing provenance must remain missing.
4. Pass an explicit evaluation time into freshness checks:
   - Preflight checks evaluate freshness at input validation.
   - Final checks evaluate freshness at publication.
   Preserve the existing time limits. A long-running calculation whose inputs expire must fail safely.
5. Apply active-price freshness gates to evidence actually used in fitting. Unused raw records must not independently fail publication.
   Required evidence coverage must still be enforced. If excluding an observation leaves a required team or market family without usable evidence, fail preflight with a specific explanation.
6. Treat a validated settled result as a settlement fact, not an active price that must update every five minutes. A closed market without a definitive result is not a settlement fact.
7. Preserve the existing treatment of wide win-total books:
   - Do not use their midpoint as an observation.
   - Record when a missing value is interpolated.
   - Record settlement anchors separately.
   - Retain raw records for audit.
8. Remove the elimination-market bypass that consumes classified bids plus one cent without respecting evidence eligibility.
   Preserve the existing pricing transformation for eligible observations in this stage. If eligibility leaves required elimination evidence missing, fail preflight. Do not invent replacement prices or enable another model.
9. Persist a compact per-observation trace showing the role, eligibility decision, exclusion reasons, and actual transformation used. Keep raw provider responses and credentials out of user-facing errors.
10. Keep provider-specific timestamp changes isolated so generic evidence consumers do not silently acquire different semantics.
