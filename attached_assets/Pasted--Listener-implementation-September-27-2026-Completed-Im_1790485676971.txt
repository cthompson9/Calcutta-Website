## Remaining live checks and limits

After separately authorized deployment, verify ticket launch, pairing, website nominations and acknowledgments. Then perform an explicitly authorized short Recall capture in a test auction to validate device permissions, credential validity, and recording timestamp alignment. Simulations do not prove those live behaviors.

The speech parser remains deliberately bounded: explicit sale phrases, exact names/aliases, standard numeric or English whole-dollar amounts, equal splits, and numeric percentages. Unsupported or ambiguous speech is held for review. Accepted sales are corrected in the website. Unresolved results and transcripts prevent auction switching; same-auction expiry can be renewed using a fresh website ticket and the existing device credential. Revoked credentials or a server-side recording flag still require operational recovery; preserve queues and verify official results.
